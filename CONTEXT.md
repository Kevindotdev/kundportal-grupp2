# Ordering

This context covers customer orders and the sales they represent, including payment-free demo orders.

## Language

**Demo order**:
An order placed through the webshop's payment-free checkout to simulate a purchase.
_Avoid_: Test sale, fake order

**Demo sale**:
Units in a successfully placed demo order, tracked separately from payment-confirmed sales.
_Avoid_: Paid sale

**Stock**:
The quantity of a product currently available to be ordered.
_Avoid_: Inventory count
